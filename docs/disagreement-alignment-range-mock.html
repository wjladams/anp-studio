Disagreement as rows: alignment + vote range

Each comparison is a table row: who vs who, an alignment score (0–100%), and a range strip of individual votes on a truncated Saaty scale with geometric median and ±1 geometric SD (pairwise).

Alignment (draft formula for this mock): 100 × (1 − ln(max/min) / ln(81)) clamped to 0–100, with 81 = 9 ÷ (1/9) as the full visual span. Identical votes → 100%. Full 1/9 vs 9 spread → 0%. Chip colors use heat-map Option 2 (traffic light): green = aligned, yellow = watch, red = unaligned.

Design notes (for when we implement)

  • Chip colors (Option 2): map alignment % to green / yellow / red bands (thresholds in the legend are illustrative).
  • Strip geometry (pairwise): position = log-lerp between 1/9 and 9. Values <1/9 or >9 clamp to the ends for drawing only.
  • Median: geometric median of positive votes for pairwise (median of logs → exp); arithmetic median for ratings.
  • ±1 SD band: pairwise in log-space (geometric SD); ratings in raw score space. Band also truncated to the visible track.
  • Dots: one per participant vote; overlapping votes can stack or use a small count badge.
  • Alignment formula above is a draft so 0% / 100% match the visual scale ends—easy to swap for a geo-SD-based score if you prefer.
  • This can replace the matrix heat, or sit as a fourth mode / detail drill-down from a hot cell.
