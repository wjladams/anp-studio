Vote range strip — options

Compare treatments for min→max (green), mean tick, and stacked votes (hybrid size + late digits). Example data matches Consensus on Goal → Alternatives (incl. two votes at 8 on A vs B).

Log Saaty track 1/9…9. Geometric mean for pairwise. Open locally: docs/vote-range-strip-options.html

D — Hybrid size + late numbers (shipped), mean on top. Green full-height bar = min→max. Dots first, then teal mean tick with white halo so it stays visible when it falls on a large stack. Dots: diameter scales from 1/5 of bar height (n=1) up to full bar height at n=5; for n≥5 the count is drawn on the (max-sized) dot.

  • Pro: Small stacks read by size; large stacks stay readable via digits.
  • Con: At n=5 both cues fire (max size + digit) — intentional redundancy.

A — Nested bands. Full-height teal rectangle = observed min→max. Smaller yellow rectangle nested inside = ±1 SD. Teal mean tick. Dots: radius scales with √n (1→4.5px … 10→~14px).

  • Pro: Clearest “range vs dispersion” nesting; matches current layout.
  • Con: When SD extends past min/max (clamped), yellow can look clipped oddly.

B — Range rail + SD capsule. Thin teal line with end caps = min→max (whisker). Soft yellow capsule = ±1 SD (can sit above/behind the rail). Mean as diamond. Dots: discrete sizes + white ring; n≥2 gets a soft glow.

  • Pro: Range vs SD use different shapes — less “two green bars” confusion.
  • Con: Slightly busier; end caps need enough vertical space.

C — Track segment fill + count badges. Teal tint fills the track only between min and max. Yellow ±1 SD drawn as a shorter rounded bar on the midline. Votes: sized by n; for n≥2 a tiny n badge sits on the dot.

  • Pro: Multiplicity is unambiguous (read the digit); good for 5–10 stacks.
  • Con: Badges need ~56px row height; can clutter dense rows.
